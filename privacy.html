Fuel Run — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Fuel Run does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Fuel Run saves your progress — coins, unlocked cars, upgrades, high scores, and daily missions — only on your own device, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and we have no access to it.

No accounts, no tracking

The game does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Third parties

Fuel Run does not integrate any third-party services that collect data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if ads or analytics are added in a future update), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
